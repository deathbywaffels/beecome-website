About me

Teacher, counsellor & play therapist

I help children become who they are meant to be, and I help parents feel supported along the way.

{{PAGE_ART:heart}}

Hi, I'm Letitia

I am a teacher with 13 years of experience, a wellness counsellor, and a qualified Therapeutic Play Practitioner.

Children are my passion. Over the years I saw how more and more learners have emotions they cannot cope with, and I had a calling to start helping them. Parents need support too, so I also give parent talks at schools and share what I know on my blog and Facebook page.

I run a practice at a primary school in Doringkloof, Centurion, and my private practice from home in Zwartkop, Centurion.
