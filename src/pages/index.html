Hello, I'm Letitia

Children are my passion

I am a teacher with 13 years of experience, a wellness counsellor, and a qualified Therapeutic Play Practitioner. I completed my Postgraduate Diploma in Play Therapy in 2023.

Over the years I saw more and more learners with emotions they cannot cope with, and I felt a calling to start helping them. But it's not just learners who need support: parents need support as well.

I run a practice at a primary school in Doringkloof, Centurion, and my private practice from home in Zwartkop, Centurion.
