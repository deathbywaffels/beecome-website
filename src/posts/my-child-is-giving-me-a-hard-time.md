Let's look at the situation differently. **A child's intention is never to give you a hard time.** The reason kids are "acting out" is because they need one of the following:

1. **Connection:** to be accepted, for an adult to delight in who he or she is.
2. **Someone who understands:** respond to the child's needs and notice how he or she feels.
3. **A reaction:** curiosity to see how you are going to react. Remember that we need to reinforce boundaries when this happens; kids are made to test boundaries.
4. **Protection:** the need to feel safe, and help to regulate emotions when necessary.

> Next time your child "acts out", ask yourself: *which of these four does my child need right now?*
