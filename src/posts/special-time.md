Many parents experience problems at home with their children: attention-seeking behaviour, stealing sweets from the cupboard, and so on.

We live in a rushed era, and it isn't always possible to spend a lot of quality time with the kids. Technology, work and stress all leave parents tired or "too busy". But kids crave one-on-one time with their parents. **They need to feel "seen" and "understood".**

## So here is an idea: add at least 10 minutes of special time a day

Choose a time when you are available and can leave your phone and any other distractions alone for 10 minutes. Give your child your undivided attention and do whatever **they** choose to do, whether it's playing dress-up, kicking a ball or singing a song. For 10 minutes your child decides what they need, and you just go along.

> 10 minutes of special time is worth more than 1 hour of sitting next to each other in front of the TV.

Add this to your daily routine and watch your child thrive at home and at school!

## Ideas for bigger special time

**Go somewhere free:** park · library · nature · walk or hike · bike ride · fishing

**Go somewhere fun:** movies · sporting event · book store · laser tag · theme park

**Stay-at-home fun:** make a craft · play anything your child wishes · bake cookies · play cards or a board game

**Go somewhere educational:** museum · take a class together · theatre
