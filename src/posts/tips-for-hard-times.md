*I found this on Facebook and do not take credit for it. The author is unknown.* But maybe you are struggling, or know someone who is going through a hard time right now. Here are some things that can help:

- **Shower.** Not a bath, a shower. Use water as hot or cold as you like. You don't even need to wash. Just get under the water and let it run over you for a while. Sit on the floor if you have to.
- **Moisturise everything.** Use whatever lotion you like, all over your body.
- **Put on clean, comfortable clothes.**
- **Drink cold water.** Use ice. Add some mint or lemon for an extra boost.
- **Clean something.** It doesn't have to be big: one drawer, five dirty dishes, a load of laundry, the bathroom sink.
- **Blast music.** Something upbeat, loud and full of energy. Sing and dance to it, even if you think you're bad at both.
- **Make food.** Not just a quick snack: take the time to make food, even if it's beans on toast. Add something special, like a soft-boiled egg or some veggies. You'll feel like you accomplished something.
- **Make something.** Write a short story or a poem, draw, colour, fold origami, crochet, knit or sculpt. Even if you don't think you're good at it. Create.
- **Go outside.** Take a walk. Sit in the grass. Look at the clouds. Smell flowers. Put your hands in the soil.
- **Call someone.** A loved one, a friend, a family member, or a chat service. If you can't call, text or email. Even if you don't say much, listen. It helps.
- **Cuddle your pets** if you have them. Take pictures of them. Talk to them.

It may seem small or silly to some, but this list keeps people alive.

> At your absolute best you won't be good enough for the wrong people. But at your worst, you'll still be worth it to the right ones. Remember that. Keep holding on.

If you feel like people don't care because no one is reaching out to you, remember that they may be feeling the same as you! And in case nobody has told you today: **you are loved, and you are worth your weight in gold and then some.** So be kind to yourself, and most of all, keep pushing on.
