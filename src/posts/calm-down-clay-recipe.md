Squishing, rolling and squeezing clay is wonderfully calming, and the lavender makes it even more soothing. Make a batch together and keep it ready for when big feelings show up.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup flour
- 2 tablespoons cream of tartar
- ½ cup salt
- 1 tablespoon oil
- Food colouring
- 1 cup boiling water
- 10 drops lavender essential oil

## Method

1. Combine the flour, salt and cream of tartar in a bowl. Mix well.
2. Mix a few drops of food colouring into the boiling water and add to the bowl.
3. Add the essential oil drops, mix everything well and knead with your hands until it is no longer sticky.
4. Once it has fully cooled, wrap it in cling wrap and put it in an airtight container.
5. Store at room temperature.

> The clay keeps for **6 months**.
